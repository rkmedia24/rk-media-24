Privacy Policy — RK MEDIA 24

Last updated: 11 November 2026 · RK MEDIA 24 (com.rkmedia24.news)

1. What this app does

RK MEDIA 24 is a news app. It shows news articles from Bangladesh and around the world. News updates automatically when new articles are published.

2. Information we collect

We collect very little information:

  • News reading counts: When you read an article, a view count is increased (one view per device per article per day). No personal identity is attached to this count.
  • Likes: When you tap Like on an article, that choice is stored on your own device and a like count is increased. No personal identity is attached.
  • Admin login: Only the app owner can log in as admin (with an email and a password) to post news. Regular readers never need to log in.

We do not collect your name, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information. We do not sell any information to anyone.

3. Third-party services

The app uses these services to work:

  • Firebase (Google): stores the news articles and the view/like counts, and lets the app fetch new articles automatically. See Google Firebase Privacy.
  • News sources: article headlines from other newspapers (for example Prothom Alo and BBC Bangla) are shown with a link to the original source. Tapping a source link opens that newspaper's website in your browser.

4. Children

The app shows general news and is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

5. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this same page.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy: email jolhash096@gmail.com or contact RK MEDIA 24 through its Facebook page (Raj Khan) or YouTube channel (@rk_media_360).
